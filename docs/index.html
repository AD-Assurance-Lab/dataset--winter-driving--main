Dual-Module Architecture

The dataset combines robust perception labeling across diverse winter corridors with high-fidelity vehicle dynamics testing at MCity.

Module 1: MI-Snow1000

Lane Detection on Snow-Covered Roads

Ego-lane boundaries annotated on dashcam frames from over 1,000 miles of winter driving in Michigan, Colorado and Wyoming. Where snow buries the paint, the boundaries follow the drivable corridor shown by tire tracks and plow lines; where no boundary can be placed with confidence, the frame has no lanes labeled and the correct output is no lane.

  • 4,888 annotated frames, 1280x720 (TuSimple format)
  • Sequence-disjoint benchmark split: 3,105 / 887 / 337 frames
  • Bare, partly covered and fully snow-covered roads, with glare

Module 2: MCity-WSPI Physics

Synchronized Vehicle Dynamics & Friction

Designed for physics-informed NeRFs, Gaussian Splatting, and vehicle-slip estimation. Recorded on a Ford Mustang Mach-E platform under controlled maneuvers on prepared winter surfaces.

  • Synchronized 25Hz images & high-rate CAN data
  • RTK-GPS + 6-DOF IMU (Z-acceleration ride-response)
  • Direct road friction, ice % & water height via MARWIS

Physics-Informed Gaussian Splatting & Simulation Parity

By matching spatial camera frames directly to high-rate vehicle dynamics, researchers can reconstruct 3D scene geometry while incorporating localized physical attributes. For instance, the transition from bare cold pavement to snow-covered surfaces is reflected not only visually, but through a drop in MARWIS optical friction (from 0.85 to 0.28) and instant wheel slip spikes (up to 30%). This allows simulation engines to evaluate control limits with actual tire–road parity.

from tools.wspi_loader import WSPISynchronizedDataset
from torch.utils.data import DataLoader
import torchvision.transforms as T

# Define standard PyTorch pipeline
transforms = T.Compose([
    T.Resize((540, 960)),
    T.ToTensor(),
])

    metadata_csv_path="metadata/mcity_wspi/jan27-downtown-1_sync.csv",
    images_dir_path="data/mcity_wspi/jan27-downtown-1",

dataloader = DataLoader(dataset, batch_size=4, shuffle=True)
